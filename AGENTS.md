# Agent guidance

Last updated: 2026-10-10

This repository owns portable writing and Alvaro's maintained public knowledge.
Start from knowledge/INDEX.md and REFERENCES.md; preserve article drafts and
source provenance. knowledge/ holds unique generalized perspectives and synthesis;
book/course summaries live in lib/learning-notes. Operations use their technical
owners, facts and decisions their living surfaces, and continuous research its
unit folders with ordered questions, hypotheses, reasoning, method and evidence.
Preserve unread URLs and missing-source pointers in SOURCES.md; do not infer
that a pointer was ingested. Raw originals belong in the authorized private
personal Drive account. Verify remote bytes before removing migrated originals.
Goals remain in Notion, linked from ROADMAP when a current owner is established.
Use YYYY/MM-DD for dated evidence and at most two lines per change in logs.
Preserve user changes; this source migration does not refresh financial facts,
publish articles, update runtime installations or rewrite Git history.
